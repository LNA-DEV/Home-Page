# Tag and category pages

Design: `docs/concepts/tag-pages.md`.

- **Every taxonomy and term page is `noindex, follow` and in no sitemap** — post tags, categories and photo tags, in all three languages, pagination included. `layouts/partials/robots-indexable.html` is the one predicate: `head.html` asks it for the meta robots and `layouts/_default/sitemap.xml` for its page list, so no page can be submitted and noindexed at once (it covers `robotsNoIndex` and `gallery-page-hidden` too). A taxonomy page is the one noindexed kind that says `follow`. **Never a robots.txt `Disallow`** for them: a crawler that may not fetch a page never sees its `noindex`. URLs, menus and the post-tag feeds are unchanged, and the append-only golden list keeps the old URLs.
- **Photo tags are a taxonomy of their own, `phototags`**, published at `/<lang>/gallery/tags/<tag>/` through `permalinks` in `hugo.yaml` — not the posts' `tags`, whose ~70 curated, per-language topics ~600 English hashtags would drown. `hugo.yaml` declares `taxonomies`, and **declaring the key replaces Hugo's defaults**, which is why `tags` and `categories` are restated there.
- **The photo content adapter sets them**: `params.phototags` = the photo's lowercased `.Tags`, nothing for an archive photo. Two things are load-bearing. They go **in `params`** — as a top-level `.AddPage` key, where `aliases`, `build` and `translationKey` go, they are ignored without a word. And they are **lowercased**: 12 tags are stored in two spellings across photos, Hugo merges them into one term and titles it after whichever photo it met first. No threshold and no ignore list — every tag of a non-archive photo gets a page (599 today, 260 of them with a single photo, `photo` and `photography` included), which is acceptable because the pages are noindexed.
- `content/phototags/_index.<lang>.md` carries the translated title and description and cascades `outputs: [HTML]`. **One per language**: a cascade reaches only its own language, and a missing stub brings ~600 feeds back in that one.
- Templates: `layouts/phototags/term.html` — a grid of the photo page's related-strip tiles (the existing 600px variant, the title, a link to the photo page; portfolio first, then newest), deliberately no lightbox — and `layouts/phototags/taxonomy.html`, every tag with its count, reached from the term pages' breadcrumb and from the text-link row under the gallery home's cards. **A term page's `.Path` is `/phototags/<tag>`, not `/gallery/…`**, so `head.html`'s `/gallery` check does not load `main.scss` or the lightbox there; nothing on it needs them, but that is the first thing to change if the lightbox is ever wanted.
- The photo page links each tag through `.GetTerms "phototags"`, matched on the term's `.Title` lowered (Hugo capitalises term titles; the terms went in lowercase), so a link exists exactly when its page does and an archive photo's tags stay plain. The lightbox popup keeps its tags as plain text.
- `gallery-meta.html` **stops the build on a tag that spells a model's slug or name** (with hyphens, spaces or neither), whatever the record's visibility — a tag would otherwise carry a name around `visibility:` and onto a page titled with it. A tripwire for the obvious spellings, not a guarantee.
- **Tag pages are not part of the URL-stability promise.** They are in no sitemap and no feed, so they never enter `tests/golden/published-urls.txt`; a photo tag's page exists only while some non-archive photo carries the tag. Tests: `tests/static/tags.spec.ts`, and the robots and sitemap blocks of `html.spec.ts` / `feeds.spec.ts`.
