{{- /* storySpecies.html A species card linking to its dex page, with how many photos of it the dex holds (docs/concepts/gallery-project-stories.md §5.3). Usage: {{< storySpecies id="osprey" >}} Params: id (required) the dex SLUG — the dex's own identity, as in its URLs The count is the dex's: every photo of the species, archive included, like the dex page itself. "html" $html) | safeHTML -}}
