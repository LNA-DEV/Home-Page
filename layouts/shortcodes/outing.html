{{- /* outing.html Text for one capture day of a journal (docs/concepts/gallery-project-stories.md §4, §7). Usage: {{< outing date="2026-08-09" title="A morning at the bog" area="nicklheimer-filzen" >}} Before sunrise, fog on the bog lake … {{< /outing >}} Params: date (required) YYYY-MM-DD, a day this project has photos from title the entry's heading; without it the day's dex species name the entry, else its first photo's title area a slug from this project's `areas:` (data/galleryAreas.yaml); the entry then gets a small map beside its text lead the UUID of the photo the entry opens with; default its first size full | medium | compact — overrides the size the day would get from its text and photo count It renders nothing where it stands: the journal is built from the photos, one entry per capture day, and this only adds text to a day.
