Progressive enhancement, because the pills are buttons and only JS can compute the intersection: the real checkboxes are rendered as a plain list and are what a plain POST would carry. assets/js/newsletter.js hides that list, reveals the pills, and submits to Listmonk's public API so the visitor stays in the panel.
