{{- /* Project override of PaperMod's figure.html. Identical markup to upstream — class/align, link, alt, caption, title, attr/attrlink all behave the same — except that a `src` pointing at a page resource is resolved and resized through responsive-image.html instead of being linked at its original size. Explicit width/height attributes still win over the computed ones; external URLs and SVGs pass through untouched.
