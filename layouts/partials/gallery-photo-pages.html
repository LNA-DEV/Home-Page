{{- /* Content adapter: one page per gallery photo. All three languages, always — the same call the dex makes. `title`, `alt` and `description` are {en, de, sv} maps in data/gallery.yaml now, each falling back to English, so a German page with English prose is a German page whose prose has not been written yet, not a duplicate: Hugo links the three through `translationKey`, head.html and sitemap.xml emit the hreflang cluster on their own, and Google reads such a cluster as alternates. Generating a page only once a translation exists would instead send a /de/ visitor to /en/ for most photos. There is one adapter file per language because Hugo scopes an adapter to the language of its filename — a bare _content.gotmpl produces English pages only. The slug is language-neutral, so the three share a URL segment and no URL moved when the other two were switched on. Every photo gets a page, archive included: only four of 648 carry no title, no alt, no species and no tags, which is far too few to justify a threshold. `aliases` is a TOP-LEVEL key of AddPage, not a param (verified on Hugo 0.166).
