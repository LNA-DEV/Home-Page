{{- /* story/people.html The names of the people in a story photo, for its caption on the portraits page (docs/gallery/portraits.md), where they take the place of the EXIF line. Names come from model-lookup.html and nowhere else, so its rule holds here too: a slug it has no entry for prints nothing, and the name is never derived from the slug. On the portraits page every photo's people are `public` by construction (gallery-story-data.html). Context: a collect-images item. Returns a slice of strings. */ -}} {{- $lang := site.Language.Lang -}} {{- $people := partialCached "model-lookup.html" (dict "lang" $lang) $lang -}} {{- $out := slice -}} {{- range .Models | default slice -}} {{- with index $people . }}{{ $out = $out | append .name }}{{ end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return $out -}}
