From the bog on the valley floor to the rock above the forest: every day out with the camera is one entry here.

{{< outing date="2026-09-12" title="Ibex above the forest" area="riesenkopf" >}}
A young alpine ibex on a limestone rock at the edge of a cliff, then a juvenile that stepped onto the pale outcrop and looked straight into the camera.
{{< /outing >}}

{{< outing date="2026-08-09" title="A morning at the bog" area="nicklheimer-filzen" lead="7b147fb0-f667-4fec-9abf-a612fba46a69" >}}
Before sunrise, fog on the bog lake. A white-tailed eagle hunted low over the water, an osprey watched from the bare tip of a spruce, and a grey heron claimed the highest branch of a birch.

Once the sun cleared the ridge, everything turned gold: three mallards banking below the forest, a roe deer at the woodland edge, ears raised.
