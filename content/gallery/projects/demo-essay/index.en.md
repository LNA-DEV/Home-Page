From the bog lakes on the valley floor to the limestone above the forest: this series collects the animals of the Inn valley near Rosenheim. Birds of prey hunting in the morning fog, a young ibex on the rocks, and a lot of small things seen up close.

{{< chapter title="The bog at dawn" subtitle="White-tailed eagles and ospreys hunting in the morning fog." area="nicklheimer-filzen" >}}

Before sunrise, fog lies on the bog lake, and the forest behind it fades into blue-grey haze. Then a white-tailed eagle comes in low over the water, hunting.

{{< storyPhoto id="989b3223-3c86-4637-947e-d0b38e221a77" layout="full" caption="white-tailed eagle skimming a fog-covered bog lake" />}}

{{< storyPhoto id="0bb3b7dc-a6fe-46d9-9718-cedb87164962" layout="left" >}}
It stays close to the surface, wings outstretched and head turned down towards the water, slipping in and out of the drifting fog. Behind it, rank after rank of spruce and birch fades into the haze.
{{< /storyPhoto >}}

{{< storyPhotos ids="576c11d6-6ee7-493b-a512-e36f6218cdb1, e0a971e5-cf10-485a-b462-d4730f1375ef" layout="pair" >}}

When the sun clears the ridge, the light turns gold, and the eagle glows against the dark forest on the mountainside.

{{< storyPhoto id="d4c0b96a-4d85-41a7-8ff1-a687b49c7dca" layout="right" >}}
The eagle was not alone. An osprey used the bare tip of a spruce above the birches as its lookout, before it lifted off and glided past a sunlit mountain farm.
