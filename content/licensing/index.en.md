I strongly believe in the copyleft and open source movement. Therefore the licenses on this page are mostly liberal.

## Website

The website code its open source under the MIT License. This license is very permissive it basically only asks for acknowledging that I build (or partly build) this page and template.

## Text Content

Text content like blog posts are licensed under CC-BY-ND. The no derivatives part is added in the text realm to not allow editing my texts to give them another meaning.

## Photography

### Default License

Most images are licensed under CC BY-ND but you need to check for each image individually. The images can have different licenses because of a multitude or reasons. Like for example if they are posted on pages with requirements for specific licenses. Or another example would be if I do a portrait and the person photographed does not consent to Creative Commons use. Which is a totally valid request. Therefore I urge you to check the image license in the info panel or the metadata of the file.

> If you cannot find any license information assume it is under copyright. Feel free to contact me in unclear cases!

### How to Check an Image's License

Each image in the [gallery](/en/gallery/) includes structured metadata with its license information. You can inspect the image's EXIF data or press the i button to get detailed information about an image.
